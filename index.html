Research

My research interests are world models under dynamics shift, deep reinforcement learning for wireless communications, and 3D point-cloud learning. My bachelor’s thesis, with Ms. Nguyen Thai Anh, asks when a world model’s uncertainty points at the errors that actually change decisions.

I care about whether a method does what it claims, so most of my work is diagnostic: seed-averaged experiments, paired statistics and preregistered hypotheses. At AILOGY I build document AI and LLM systems that run in production.

    When Does Group Consistency Help? A Diagnostic Audit of Partition-Induced Regularization for TLS Leaf–Wood Segmentation

    P. Q. Minh, T. V. Vu, A. T. Nguyen, T. T. Thanh, V. N. Q. Bao, N. H. Tu

    Manuscript in preparation, first author

    Summary

    An audit of group-consistency regularization on Point Transformer v3 across 43M+ TLS points from 30 tropical trees. Gains are tested at tree level over a 5-seed panel with paired bootstrap confidence intervals, Wilcoxon signed-rank tests and TOST equivalence margins. Zero-shot transfer to four external datasets (FOR-instance, ForestSemantic, NIBIO MLS, Wytham Woods) isolates label-ontology effects, and a downstream QSM analysis shows how the spatial pattern of errors changes woody-volume estimates independently of point-wise mIoU.

  4. 2025

    Fruit Ripeness Detection: A Comparative Analysis of State-of-the-Art YOLO Models

    P. Q. Minh, L. N. Tan, N. H. Tu, A. T. Nguyen

    IEEE RIVF 2025, Ho Chi Minh City, December 2025, first author

    Summary

    A benchmark of YOLOv5 and YOLOv8 variants with ResNet backbones on a curated multi-condition fruit dataset with custom augmentation. The best model reaches 0.757 mAP on the target classes, a 5.6% improvement, at under 30 ms per image for real-time edge use.

OFIVI: an AI-native digital office

OFIVI brings scattered work tools (file storage, documents, spreadsheets, slides, team chat, calendar and tasks) into one workspace. It is installed on the organization’s own infrastructure, so data never leaves it, and every AI answer cites its sources so people can check them.

  • An assistant that answers questions over the organization’s own documents, summarizes, compares two versions of a text and flags risky content. It can act in the other tools, but it proposes first and writes only after the user approves.
  • Central document storage with hybrid keyword and semantic search that returns the exact passage, with its source.
  • AI writing, spreadsheet analysis in plain language, and documents turned into slide decks, with DOCX and PPTX export.
  • AI notebooks that pull many sources together and explore them through mind maps, summary reports and deep research.
  • Team chat and calls on web and mobile, plus a Today view, calendar, tasks and notes.
  • Meeting minutes, in progress: Vietnamese speech recognition with speaker separation that drafts conclusions, tasks and deadlines.
